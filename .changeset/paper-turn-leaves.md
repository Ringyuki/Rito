The paper turn no longer loses its leaf when another turn cuts in: turned back, the same leaf slows, stops and comes back; turned on, it carries on down by itself while the next lifts, several in the air at once and none through another; a finger takes a leaf in the air by the point it touched. `TransitionPhysics.begins` says where a turn that cuts into another begins, and a `TransitionPage` says which `spread` it is.
