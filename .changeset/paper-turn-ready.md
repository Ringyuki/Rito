An effect may have a `prepare()`, called when it is set: the paper turn hands the GPU its programs there without waiting, so the first frame of the first turn does not compile them, and a turn begun before they are compiled is drawn as `cover` to its end. On displays of two device pixels or more to a CSS pixel its canvas is not multisampled, which took a third of every frame, and a leaf's edge is softened in the shader.
