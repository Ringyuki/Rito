Laying a chapter out again at another size, as for every size of a window being dragged, lays it out only through the spread the reader stays on and the page after it, which is what the spread shown and whether a page follows it read. The rest of the chapter is laid out when a turn reaches it or a search, a text interaction or a page table reads it, and every spread is the one laying out the whole chapter shows.
