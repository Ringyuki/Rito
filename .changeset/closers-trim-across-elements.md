A closing bracket or quote that overflows a line by half its width is trimmed to keep it on the line when the line crosses an element boundary and when the closer ends its element, as Chromium does since version 153: such a line no longer sends a whole note or the run after an annotated name down to the next line, and a trimmed closer keeps the end padding of the box it closes.
