Emphasis marks paint as Chromium paints them. `text-emphasis-style`, `text-emphasis-color`, `text-emphasis-position` and the `text-emphasis` shorthand (with their `-webkit-` spellings, and the `-epub-` spellings of all but the position) reach the engine; a character that takes a mark gets one, set in its face at half its size, outside its em box on the side the position names (a vertical line's right unless it says left), an upright mark centred on it down the column. Marks over a line push it down by what the room under the previous line's text does not absorb, marks under one grow its box, and the next line rises into that growth by the room above its own text.
