A finger's speed is measured as touch toolkits measure it: from when it came down, over its last 100 ms, and none once it has held still 40 ms. A short drag held still before it lifts goes back instead of turning the page as if flicked, and so does a nudge after the finger has rested on the page; a flick lifted while moving still turns it.
